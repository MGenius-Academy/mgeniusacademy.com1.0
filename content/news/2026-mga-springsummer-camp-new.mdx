MGenius Academy (MGA) is offering a 2026 Spring & Summer Day Camp for students in grades K-6, described as "a Journey of Creativity and Learning" featuring "engaging activities, interactive projects, and endless opportunities to unleash your imagination."

![2026 MGA Spring & Summer Camp](/images/post_2026-mga-springsummer-camp-new/iShot_2026-04-13_17.40.51-231x300.png)

## Schedule & Hours

- **Daily Hours:** Monday-Friday, 8:30 a.m. to 3:00 p.m.
- **Extended Pickup:** Optional extension available until 5 p.m.

## Key Details

The camp is designed for "all skill levels" and aims to inspire creativity through hands-on learning experiences. The program emphasizes interactive STEM and creative projects.

![MGA Spring & Summer Camp activities](/images/post_2026-mga-springsummer-camp-new/he-cheng-0407-1024x663.jpg)

## Registration

Prospective participants can reserve spots via a Google Form link provided on the page. The announcement notes that "spaces are limited."
