## A Special Spring Event for Students and Families

As education continues to evolve, more families are looking for learning experiences that go beyond traditional academics. Today, **STEM education and entrepreneurial thinking** are becoming increasingly important in helping students build creativity, confidence, and real-world problem-solving skills.

To help families explore these opportunities, **MGA hosted a Spring Open House on May 9** for students in **Grades 1–8** and their parents. The event featured hands-on student trial classes, program introductions for parents, and a special guest speaker with deep experience in independent school admissions.

Whether a child was just beginning to explore STEM or ready for more advanced competition and innovation programs, the Open House was a great opportunity to experience MGA's educational approach firsthand.

![MGA Spring Open House flyer for May 9](/images/post_mga-open-house-on-may-9-discover-stem-innovation-and-entrepreneurship-for-grades-1-8/0509Open-House-1-745x1024.jpg)

## Event Information

- **Date:** Friday, May 9
- **Location:** 288 Walnut St, Suite 300, Newton, MA 02460

## Session 1: Elementary Open House for Grades 1–4

**Time: 1:30–2:45 PM**

This session was designed for younger students who are interested in hands-on STEM learning.

### Student Experience

Students participated in:

- a **45-minute engineering trial class**
- a **25-minute fun math experience**

These activities were designed to strengthen logical thinking, creativity, and curiosity through interactive learning.

### Parent Session

While students were in class, parents learned more about:

- MGA's course system
- upcoming summer camps
- academic pathways for long-term growth

This session was ideal for families who wanted an introduction to MGA's elementary STEM and math programs.

## Session 2: Competition and Innovation Open House for Grades 4+

**Time: 3:00–4:30 PM**

This session was designed for older students who are ready to explore advanced STEM learning, project-based innovation, and entrepreneurship.

### New Featured Experience: Young Innovator and Entrepreneur Program

One of the highlights of this event was a new interactive experience inspired by students from **Babson College**, a school widely recognized for entrepreneurship education. This program combines **technology, invention, and business thinking**, helping students experience how ideas can develop into real products. Students not only worked on projects, but also began thinking about product purpose, user needs, and market value.

![Young Innovator and Entrepreneur Program experience at MGA](/images/post_mga-open-house-on-may-9-discover-stem-innovation-and-entrepreneurship-for-grades-1-8/image.png)

### Signature Program Trial Experiences

Families also had the opportunity to explore several of MGA's advanced programs:

**AI Chatbot / NeuroMaker / AI Competition Projects** — Students explored the connection between artificial intelligence, biomimicry, and innovation while learning about advanced AI project pathways.

![AI Chatbot trial experience at MGA Open House](/images/post_mga-open-house-on-may-9-discover-stem-innovation-and-entrepreneurship-for-grades-1-8/AI-Chatbot-0408-745x1024.jpg)

**3D Invention, Toy Design, and Entrepreneurship Program** — This program introduced students to 3D invention and creative product design, while encouraging entrepreneurial thinking.

**ICW Global Inventor Program** — Students learned how original ideas can be developed into strong projects for international invention competitions.

## Special Guest Speaker for Parents

A major highlight of the Open House was a featured parent talk by **Sarah W.**, a former admissions leader at **Milton Academy**.

### Parent Talk Topic: Why STEM Is Incredibly Important, Especially These Days

![Guest speaker Sarah W., former admissions leader at Milton Academy](/images/post_mga-open-house-on-may-9-discover-stem-innovation-and-entrepreneurship-for-grades-1-8/image-1.png)

Sarah W. previously served as:

- **Associate Director of Admission at Milton Academy**
- **Director of Admission at Shady Hill School**

With years of experience in independent school admissions, she brought valuable insight into how schools evaluate student potential and why STEM learning has become increasingly meaningful in today's educational environment. Her talk offered families a rare opportunity to hear directly from a former admissions professional about the growing importance of STEM in student development and school admissions.
