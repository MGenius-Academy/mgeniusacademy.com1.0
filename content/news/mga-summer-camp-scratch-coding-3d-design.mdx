MGA's Scratch Coding & 3D Design summer camp centered on "Creating Games, Designing Products, and Turning Ideas into Reality," with students designing video games, creating everyday products, baking with custom tools, and making ice cream using dry ice.

![MGA Scratch Coding & 3D Design camp](/images/post_mga-summer-camp-scratch-coding-3d-design/image-14-1024x1024.jpeg)

![Students beginning the week's projects](/images/post_mga-summer-camp-scratch-coding-3d-design/image-15-1024x1024.jpeg)

## Day 1 | MouseBot Coding & Custom Cookie Cutter Design

Students learned Scratch fundamentals through MouseBot Coding, then designed custom cookie cutters using 3D pens and Tinkercad.

![Students learning Scratch fundamentals](/images/post_mga-summer-camp-scratch-coding-3d-design/image-16-768x1024.jpeg)

## Day 2 | Scratch Snake & Creative Cookie Workshop

Students programmed the classic Snake game in Scratch, learning movement and collision detection. They then baked cookies using their designed cutters.

The week's most advanced project involved programming a Jumping Dinosaur game with jumping mechanics and obstacle generation. Students handcrafted soaps paired with their designed boxes.
