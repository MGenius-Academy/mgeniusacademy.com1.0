## Young Engineers: Light Up the House!

### Kids' Challenge: Build an Electrical Circuit

Every glowing haunted house needs a working electrical circuit! With guidance from MGA instructors, students will explore basic electronic components, learn how circuits work, and build their own lighting systems.

To make the experience appropriate for different ages, we offer two levels:

  <div className="rounded-2xl border border-neutral-200 bg-white p-5 shadow-sm">
    <p className="text-2xl font-bold text-orange-500">EP1</p>
    <p className="text-sm font-semibold text-neutral-900">Kindergarten – Grade 1</p>
    <p className="mt-3 text-sm leading-relaxed text-neutral-600">Young learners will explore the basics of electricity, identify simple electronic components, and practice connecting a circuit to light up their houses.</p>
  </div>
  <div className="rounded-2xl border border-neutral-200 bg-white p-5 shadow-sm">
    <p className="text-2xl font-bold text-violet-600">EP3</p>

    <p className="mt-3 text-sm leading-relaxed text-neutral-600">Older students will take on a more advanced circuit-building challenge. They'll test their connections, troubleshoot problems, and make adjustments until their lighting systems work as planned.</p>
  </div>
</div>

Along the way, students will practice problem-solving, logical thinking, and the hands-on skills that engineers use every day.

## Parents, It's Your Turn to Create!

### Parents' Challenge: Design and Laser-Cut the Walls

Usually, it's the kids who come to MGA for class. This time, parents get to join the learning experience too! Parents will step into our makerspace and learn to use design software to create the walls of their haunted houses.

Want to add mysterious windows, a spooky front door, or some unique Halloween details? **It's your design, so get creative!**

Once the designs are ready, we'll use MGA's laser cutter to turn digital drawings into physical pieces ready for assembly. It's a fun opportunity for parents to experience the process of turning an idea on a computer into something they can actually build.

## Teamwork Time: Bring Your Haunted House to Life!

_When kids' circuits meet parents' designs…_

Now it's time to put everything together! Children will bring their completed circuits, parents will bring their custom-designed walls, and together they'll assemble their haunted houses.

Of course, no Halloween creation is complete without decorations! We'll provide colorful pipe cleaners, pom-poms, twine, and other craft materials so families can add their own creative touches.

Will yours be a friendly ghost's cottage or a mysterious haunted mansion? Every family's creation will be a little different!

## Young Designers: Show Off Your Creations!

### The Final 30 Minutes: A Mini Design Showcase

At the end of the workshop, each child will have an opportunity to introduce their haunted house to the group. What did they create? Which part are they most proud of? Did they face any challenges along the way?

There's no need for a formal presentation. We simply want students to feel comfortable sharing their ideas and celebrating what they've accomplished.
