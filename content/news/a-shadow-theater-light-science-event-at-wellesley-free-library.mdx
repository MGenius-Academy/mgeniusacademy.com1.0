What happens when **2,000-year-old shadow theater** meets **modern light technology**?

MGenius Academy and **Ying Qu Fang – Shadow Joy Lab** teamed up for **The Great Zodiac River Race**, a unique family-friendly event that brought science, storytelling, art, and culture together. Through colorful shadow puppets, live storytelling, and an exploration of light and shadow, children and families discovered how an ancient art form can be transformed into something surprisingly modern.

## Event Details

- **Event Name:** The Great Zodiac River Race
- **Date:** Saturday, August 29, 2026
- **Time:** 2:00 PM – 4:00 PM (Eastern Time)
- **Location:** Wakelin Room, Main Library, Wellesley Free Library
- **Audience:** All Ages
- **Presented by:** MGenius Academy and Ying Qu Fang – Shadow Joy Lab
- **Sponsored by:** Friends of the Wellesley Free Libraries

## About the Event

The Great Zodiac River Race was a playful exploration of art, culture, storytelling, and the science of light. Participants watched colorful shadow puppets come alive on stage while learning how light, shadow, movement, and technology work together to create visual storytelling. The event was designed for curious minds of all ages, introducing children to both traditional shadow theater and modern STEM-based creativity.

## What Families Experienced

- Watch a colorful shadow puppet performance
- Explore the connection between light, shadow, and storytelling
- Learn how ancient shadow theater can be combined with modern technology
- Experience a creative blend of STEM, art, and culture
- Enjoy an engaging children's event at Wellesley Free Library

## Why This Event Matters

> "Meaningful learning happens when students connect creativity with real-world science."
> — MGenius Academy

The Great Zodiac River Race introduced children to the physics of light and shadow through an artistic and cultural experience. By combining traditional storytelling with modern light technology, the event helped young learners see how science can support creativity, performance, and design.
