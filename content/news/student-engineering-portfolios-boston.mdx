## Beyond the Build

MGenius Academy's EP5 program distinguishes itself by requiring students to document their STEM projects through personal websites, mirroring professional engineering practices.

## Three Student Projects

**Vincent's Bluetooth Speaker**

Vincent constructed a laser-cut wooden Bluetooth speaker and created a website functioning as a technical specification sheet. His documentation included component annotations with photographs and explanations of the potentiometer, amplifier, battery charger, Bluetooth decoding board, and speaker driver. He labeled circuit board photos identifying USB power, ground, and audio channels.

![Vincent's laser-cut wooden Bluetooth speaker with labeled circuit board components](/images/post_student-engineering-portfolios-boston/Vincent-1-1017x1024.jpg)

**Reina's Build Journal**

Reina formatted her project documentation as a daily progress log capturing the iterative nature of engineering work:

> "Day 1: The most annoying thing was me keeping messing it up and creating giant bumps. The wires kept coming out and it took forever…"

Her honest account revealed multiple failed attempts and the frustrations inherent in building prototypes, complete with photographs of wire tangles and her final decorated speaker.

![Reina's day-by-day build log showing her iterative design process and finished speaker](/images/post_student-engineering-portfolios-boston/Reina-1017x1024.jpg)

**Brayden's Multi-Project Portfolio**

Brayden created a comprehensive portfolio website featuring multiple projects, including a Seven Segment Digital Clock and Bluetooth Speaker. His 3D-printed speaker combined a solid base with a lattice-patterned design.

![Brayden's full portfolio website featuring multiple projects, including a 3D-printed lattice-patterned speaker](/images/post_student-engineering-portfolios-boston/81151778274877_.pic_-1024x582.jpg)

## Why Documentation Matters

The program emphasizes that the work isn't done until it's documented and shared. Students develop skills in technical writing, photography, 3D modeling, laser cutting, coding, and web design simultaneously.
