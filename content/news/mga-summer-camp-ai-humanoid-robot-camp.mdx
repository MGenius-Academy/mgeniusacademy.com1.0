What happens when you give children a box full of robot parts? Will they simply assemble a toy — or build an AI companion with its own personality?

At MGA's **AI Humanoid Robot Camp**, students didn't receive a finished robot. Instead, they started with hundreds of individual parts and gradually transformed them into fully functional AI humanoid robots through engineering, programming, and artificial intelligence.

## Building a Robot, One Screw at a Time

Every great invention begins with a strong foundation. Students carefully assembled the robot frame by installing servo motors, connecting wires, and attaching structural components one piece at a time. Along the way, they discovered how each part contributes to the robot's movement.

![Students assembling the frame of their AI humanoid robot](/images/post_mga-summer-camp-ai-humanoid-robot-camp/IMG_1419-1-1024x768.jpg)

As they built, students explored questions such as:

- Why can a robot raise its arms?
- How does it walk, turn, and balance?
- What role does each servo motor play?

Watching their robots stand up and move for the very first time was an unforgettable moment — a rewarding experience no off-the-shelf toy could ever provide.

## From Mechanical Engineering to Artificial Intelligence

Once the hardware was complete, the real adventure began. Students connected their robots to computers, uploaded programs, and gradually unlocked movement functions such as walking and turning, learning how hardware and software work together as one complete system.

They then explored two operating modes. **Remote Control Mode** let students directly control their robots' movements, including walking, turning, and waving. **AI Conversation Mode** enabled the robots to recognize speech, understand commands, and engage in natural conversations. Their robots were no longer just moving — they were interacting.

![A student directing their robot using AI conversation mode](/images/post_mga-summer-camp-ai-humanoid-robot-camp/IMG_1634-1024x768.jpg)

## Every Robot Has Its Own Personality

Artificial intelligence is what truly brought each robot to life. Students customized their robots by choosing a unique voice, a distinct personality, and different conversation styles. Each robot became a reflection of its creator — some humorous, some gentle and thoughtful, others energetic and playful.

![A student customizing their robot's voice and personality](/images/post_mga-summer-camp-ai-humanoid-robot-camp/3A225C7D383FD521C972CEFD62CC8B8A-1024x573.jpg)

Through this process, students discovered that AI is much more than answering questions — it can create meaningful, personalized interactions.

## Creativity Brings Robots to Life

With the core functions complete, students explored creative possibilities. Using remote controllers, they programmed their robots to perform different movements, picked their favorite music, and even taught their robots to dance. As music filled the classroom and robots danced alongside their creators, engineering transformed into creativity, and each robot became more than a project — it became an AI companion built entirely by the students.

![Students teaching their robots to dance](/images/post_mga-summer-camp-ai-humanoid-robot-camp/IMG_1657-1-1024x768.jpg)

## Every Success Begins with Persistence

Building a humanoid robot is no easy task. Sometimes a single misplaced screw meant taking the robot apart and rebuilding it. A loose wire required careful troubleshooting, and programs often needed multiple rounds of testing before everything worked correctly.

![Students troubleshooting their robot builds together](/images/post_mga-summer-camp-ai-humanoid-robot-camp/IMG_1581-1024x768.jpg)

Through every challenge, students experienced the authentic engineering design process — testing, improving, and trying again. More importantly, they developed patience, attention to detail, resilience, and confidence.

## AI Is More Than a Chatbot

When people hear the term "AI," many immediately think of ChatGPT or virtual assistants. This week, however, our students experienced AI from a completely different perspective. They discovered that AI isn't limited to a chat window on a screen — combined with mechanical engineering, servo motors, programming, and networking, AI gives robots the ability to understand commands, recognize voices, move intelligently, and communicate naturally.

This is the vision of AI education at MGA: we want students not only to use AI, but also to understand AI, create with AI, and apply AI to solve real-world problems. Because the future belongs not only to AI users, but to the innovators who build it.

## Sharing Their Creations with Confidence

During the camp's Closing Ceremony, students proudly introduced their AI humanoid robots to their families and fellow campers. They demonstrated walking, dancing, and voice interaction while explaining the personalities, voices, and unique characteristics they had designed for their robots. Each presentation reflected not only technical achievement, but also creativity, problem-solving, and confidence.

From assembling a collection of unfamiliar parts to proudly presenting a fully functioning AI robot, students gained much more than an engineering project — they gained confidence in their ability to create.

A heartfelt thank you to all of our amazing campers for their curiosity, dedication, and hard work, and to all of our families for your trust and support. We look forward to welcoming everyone back to MGA as we continue exploring the exciting worlds of artificial intelligence, robotics, engineering, and innovation together.
