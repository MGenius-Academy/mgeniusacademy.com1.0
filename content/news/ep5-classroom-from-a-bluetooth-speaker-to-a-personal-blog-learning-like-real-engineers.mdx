MGA's EP5 classroom students completed personal Bluetooth speaker projects that connect to phones and play music. The learning experience extended beyond construction to include documentation through project blogs with writing and images.

![Bluetooth speaker project components and assembly work](/images/post_ep5-classroom-from-a-bluetooth-speaker-to-a-personal-blog-learning-like-real-engineers/image-2-1024x357.png)

## Educational Philosophy

The academy emphasizes: **Hands-on + Critical Thinking + Communication**.

> "Students should not only learn how to build something — they should also learn how to explain it."

## Project Phases

**Electronic Components Learning**

Students studied component functions including:

- Bluetooth decoding boards
- Amplifier boards
- Battery charging modules
- Speaker and knob control modules

![Electronic components including Bluetooth boards and amplifier modules](/images/post_ep5-classroom-from-a-bluetooth-speaker-to-a-personal-blog-learning-like-real-engineers/image-4-1024x222.png)

**Circuit Assembly**

Students connected components through soldering. One participant noted:

> "Ninety per cent of the project was probably soldering all the wires to the speaker and boards!"

![Soldering circuit work on speaker components](/images/post_ep5-classroom-from-a-bluetooth-speaker-to-a-personal-blog-learning-like-real-engineers/image.png)

**Case Design**

Using 3D printing or laser cutting, students created personalized cases by measuring dimensions and planning button/port openings while iterating on designs.

![3D printed or laser-cut speaker case designs](/images/post_ep5-classroom-from-a-bluetooth-speaker-to-a-personal-blog-learning-like-real-engineers/image-5-1024x241.png)

**Documentation**

Students created online portfolio project blogs documenting their thinking process, challenges, and problem-solving journey alongside the final product.

![Student project blog documentation with photos](/images/post_ep5-classroom-from-a-bluetooth-speaker-to-a-personal-blog-learning-like-real-engineers/image-1-1024x509.png)

## Core Outcome

The academy aims for students to confidently articulate both their creation and the development process, recognizing that real engineers communicate ideas and document processes beyond simply making things function.
